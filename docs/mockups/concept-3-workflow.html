From regulatory text to internal gap — one evidence trail at a time.

This concept explains the skill through its actual workflow: capture the regulatory requirement, gather internal evidence, assess the fit, preserve the version, and keep reassessment additive. It is more narrative and method-teaching than the other two concepts.

Read the skill View repository
Step 01

Capture the requirement

Preserve verbatim regulatory text before interpretation. Split compound passages into atomic requirements that can actually be tested.

Step 02

Find internal evidence

Assess the organisation’s policies, standards, and linked documents for exact support. Neighbouring references become dependencies to investigate, not automatic proof.

Step 03

Record the conclusion

One requirement may be supported by many internal sources. The structure is intentionally repeated so the workbook remains useful in real Excel review, filtering, and pivots.

Step 04

Reassess without overwriting history

New evidence can change the conclusion. The method keeps prior rows and versions intact so the reasoning path remains auditable.
