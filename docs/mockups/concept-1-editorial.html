What it does

Turns regulatory assessment into a repeatable evidence workflow.

The skill processes regulations sequentially, splits compound passages into atomic requirements, preserves the exact wording that matters, and then tests internal policy evidence against each requirement without guessing from topical similarity.

  • Every claimed match requires verbatim internal evidence
  • Multiple internal standards can collectively satisfy one requirement
  • New evidence can strengthen, weaken, contradict, or overturn earlier conclusions
  • Reassessment history is preserved instead of overwritten
Why it matters

Change the model if you want. Keep the machine: method, evidence discipline, structure, and output logic.

This skill is meant for teams who need a more defensible way to assess regulations against internal standards — especially where exact wording, cumulative evidence, and auditability matter more than fast summaries.
